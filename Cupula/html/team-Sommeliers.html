SOMMELIERS

Keepers of wine and tradition

THE ART OF THE CELLAR

At La Cúpula, wine is treated not merely as a pairing, but as a dialogue between history, terroir, and emotion. Our sommeliers guide guests through selections that honor both classical appellations and contemporary makers, weaving stories in every glass served.

MAIN SOMMELIER
Elena Villagrán

Elena oversees the entire philosophy of the cellar, curating vintages that reflect elegance, depth, and narrative. Her expertise lies in old-world wines and rare Iberian heritage bottles.

JUNIOR SOMMELIER
Mateo Lérida

Supporting the cellar's service, Mateo specializes in emerging winemakers and innovative pairings, ensuring refinement with a youthful perspective.
