How to Reach Italy

By Air

Italy is well-connected globally. Direct flights are available from most major capitals. Italy's main carrier is ITA Airways.

  • Rome Fiumicino (FCO): Largest international hub, connecting to USA, Asia, and all of Europe.
  • Milan Malpensa (MXP): Main gateway for intercontinental flights in the North.
  • Venice (VCE): Ideal for accessing the North-East and Dolomites.

By Train

Excellent high-speed rail connections from neighboring countries via the Alps.

  • TGV/Frecciarossa: Direct from Paris and Lyon to Milan/Turin (approx 6-7h).
  • EuroCity: Direct from Zurich, Geneva, and Munich to Milan, Verona, and Venice.
  • Nightjet: Overnight trains from Vienna, Salzburg, and Munich to Rome, Florence, and Venice.

By Car & Sea

Accessible via tunnels (Mont Blanc, Frejus) from France or Brenner Pass from Austria. Ferries connect major ports like Genoa, Civitavecchia, and Bari with Spain, Greece, Croatia, and Tunisia.

Visa Information

Italy is a Schengen Area member. EU citizens travel with ID. Citizens of USA, Canada, Australia, Japan, etc. do not need a visa for stays under 90 days for tourism. Check ETIAS requirements (2025).
