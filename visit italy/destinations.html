Discover Italian Wonders

From the snow-capped Alps in the north to the sun-drenched ruins of the south, explore the diverse regions that make Italy a timeless masterpiece.

ALPI

Northern Italy

Experience the perfect blend of cosmopolitan fashion, dramatic alpine landscapes, and romantic waterways in Italy's prosperous north.

Veneto

Venice

Built on more than 100 small islands in a lagoon in the Adriatic Sea, Venice has no roads, just canals. Marvel at the Renaissance and Gothic architecture, and explore the bustling waterways that make this floating city completely unique.

Lombardy

Milan

A global capital of fashion and design, Milan is home to high-end restaurants, exclusive boutiques, and magnificent architecture. It is the fast-paced economic heart of Italy, seamlessly blending history with modernity.

More to Explore in the North

Lake

Lake Como

Set against the foothills of the Alps, known for its dramatic scenery and beautiful lakeside villas.
Mountain

The Dolomites

A stunning mountain range offering world-class skiing in winter and breathtaking hiking trails in summer.
City

Verona

The city of Romeo and Juliet, featuring a beautifully preserved Roman amphitheater still used for operas.
STORIA

Central Italy

Journey through the rolling hills of Tuscany and the ancient streets of Rome, where Western civilization and art were forever shaped.

Lazio

Rome

The Eternal City is a sprawling metropolis where 3,000 years of globally influential art, architecture, and culture are on display. From the ruins of the Roman Empire to the bustling modern piazzas, Rome is an open-air museum.

Tuscany

Florence

The undisputed cradle of the Renaissance. Florence's streets are filled with masterpieces by Michelangelo, Botticelli, and Da Vinci. It is a city that looks almost unchanged from the 15th century, wrapped in rolling Tuscan hills.

More to Explore in the Center

Monument

Pisa

Home to the world-famous Leaning Tower and the beautiful Piazza dei Miracoli.
Historic Town

Siena

A medieval masterpiece known for its fan-shaped central square, Piazza del Campo.
Village

Assisi

A stunning hill town in Umbria, famous as the birthplace of St. Francis and its magnificent Basilica.
SOLE

Southern Italy

Discover dramatic coastlines, ancient Greek ruins, and the vibrant, passionate street life of the Mediterranean's most colorful region.

Campania

Naples

Raw, vibrant, and energetic. Naples is the birthplace of pizza and offers an incredibly rich cultural heritage set against the dramatic backdrop of Mount Vesuvius. It's a city of narrow alleys, hidden palazzos, and unmatched culinary traditions.

Campania

The Amalfi Coast

A spectacularly beautiful stretch of rugged coastline dotted with pastel-colored fishing villages like Positano and Amalfi. Terraced lemon groves meet sheer cliffs plunging into the azure waters of the Tyrrhenian Sea.

More to Explore in the South

Ruins

Pompeii

The ancient Roman city perfectly preserved under volcanic ash from the 79 AD eruption of Vesuvius.
Cave Town

Matera

Known for its ancient cave dwellings (Sassi) carved directly into the rocky hillsides of Basilicata.
Village

Alberobello

Famous for its unique trulli buildings, characterized by conical roofs made of dry stone.
MARE

The Islands

Cross the sea to Sicily and Sardinia, where unique traditions, pristine beaches, and cross-cultural histories await.

Sicily

Palermo

The bustling capital of Sicily is an intoxicating mix of Arab-Norman architecture, vibrant street markets, and incredible street food. It is a true crossroads of civilizations right in the heart of the Mediterranean.

Sardinia

Cagliari

Sardinia's historic capital offers a beautiful hilltop citadel, sun-drenched palazzos, and sweeping views of the sea. It serves as the perfect gateway to the island's legendary pristine beaches and mysterious Nuragic ruins.

More to Explore in the Islands

Volcano

Mount Etna

Europe's highest and most active volcano, dominating the eastern coast of Sicily.
Ruins

Valley of the Temples

One of the most outstanding examples of Greater Greece art and architecture in Agrigento.
Coastline

Costa Smeralda

Sardinia's emerald coast, renowned for its turquoise waters and fine white sand beaches.
