Curated Journeys

Follow our expertly designed itineraries to experience the best history, art, and gastronomy Italy has to offer.

Logistics

Best Seasons Spring (Apr-May) & Fall (Sep-Oct)
Main Transport High-Speed Rail (Frecciarossa)
Pace Moderate (Lots of city walking)

Highlights

  • Private gondola ride through the narrow canals of Venice at twilight.
  • VIP access to Michelangelo’s David and the Uffizi Gallery in Florence.
  • Exclusive underground tour of the Colosseum and Roman Forum.
  • Authentic Tuscan wine tasting and culinary masterclass.
1
Venice: The Floating City
Days 1-3
Begin your journey navigating the water-bound magic of Venice. Explore the intricate mosaics of St. Mark's Basilica, wander through the labyrinthine alleys of Dorsoduro, and take a traditional vaporetto down the Grand Canal. Conclude your stay with a romantic sunset gondola ride.
2
Florence: Renaissance Rebirth
Days 4-6
Travel by high-speed train to the heart of Tuscany. Stand in awe of the Duomo's magnificent red-tiled dome, explore the vast art collections in the Uffizi Gallery, and walk across the historic Ponte Vecchio. Enjoy evenings dining in traditional Florentine trattorias.
3
Rome: Imperial Majesty
Days 7-10
Conclude your grand tour in the eternal city. Walk in the footsteps of emperors at the Colosseum and Roman Forum. Cross into Vatican City to witness the breathtaking Sistine Chapel, and ensure your return to Italy by tossing a coin into the Trevi Fountain.

Logistics

Best Seasons Autumn (For grape harvest)
Main Transport Rental Car
Pace Relaxed & Leisurely

Highlights

  • Scenic driving through the iconic cypress-lined roads of Val d'Orcia.
  • Exclusive Brunello di Montalcino wine tasting in historic cellars.
  • Exploring the medieval, fan-shaped Piazza del Campo in Siena.
  • Tasting authentic Pecorino cheese in the hilltop town of Pienza.
1
Florence: Journey Begins
Days 1-2
Start in the Renaissance capital to pick up your rental car. Spend your time admiring the Duomo's magnificent dome and enjoying the vibrant atmosphere of Piazza della Signoria before heading south into the picturesque countryside.
2
Siena: Medieval Masterpiece
Days 3-4
Drive through the Chianti region towards Siena. Stand in the center of the famous fan-shaped Piazza del Campo, visit the stunning striped cathedral, and enjoy a premium wine-tasting session in a nearby historic vineyard.
3
Val d'Orcia: Golden Harmony
Days 5-7
Journey into the quintessential Tuscan landscape. Drive along roads lined with iconic cypress trees, visit the hilltop towns of Pienza and Montalcino, and savor the world-renowned Brunello wine while watching the sunset over the rolling golden hills.

Logistics

Best Seasons Summer (Jun-Aug)
Main Transport Train (Milan) + Car (Lakes)
Pace Elegant & Sophisticated

Highlights

  • High-end shopping in Milan's Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II.
  • Classic wooden Riva boat tour across the pristine waters of Lake Como.
  • Exploring the botanical gardens of Villa Carlotta and Villa Balbianello.
  • Attending a live open-air opera in the ancient Roman Arena of Verona.
1
Milan: Metropolitan Luxury
Days 1-3
Begin in Italy's buzzing fashion capital. Marvel at the intricate Gothic architecture of the Duomo, shop in the historic Galleria, and enjoy a sophisticated evening aperitivo in the vibrant Navigli canal district.
2
Lake Como: Alpine Serenity
Days 4-6
Pick up your car and drive to the foothills of the Alps. Retreat to the aristocratic elegance of Lake Como. Explore the pearl of the lake, Bellagio, and take a boat tour to visit historic waterfront villas surrounded by dramatic mountains.
3
Verona: City of Love
Days 7-8
Conclude your trip amidst operatic grandeur. Visit Juliet's famous balcony, stroll through the bustling Piazza delle Erbe, and attend a magical evening performance at the incredibly preserved Roman amphitheater.

Logistics

Best Seasons Early Summer (May-Jun)
Main Transport Local Trains & Ferries
Pace Challenging (Steep steps)

Highlights

  • Tasting the original, authentic Margherita pizza in historic Naples.
  • Guided archaeological tour walking the ash-preserved streets of Pompeii.
  • Sailing along the dramatic, plunging cliffs of the Amalfi coastline.
  • Wandering the steep, lemon-scented alleys of Positano.
1
Naples: Chaotic Energy
Days 1-2
Dive into the vibrant flavors of the south. Start in chaotic, beautiful Naples. Eat authentic Neapolitan pizza where it was invented, explore the narrow alleys of the Spanish Quarters, and visit the National Archaeological Museum.
2
Pompeii: Frozen in Time
Day 3
Take a short train ride to step back in time to 79 AD. Walk the silent, ancient streets of Pompeii, incredibly preserved under volcanic ash, all lying under the dramatic shadow of Mount Vesuvius.
3
Amalfi Coast: Golden Sun
Days 4-6
Unwind on the spectacularly beautiful coastal cliffs. Visit the vertical, pastel-colored houses of Positano, buy handmade ceramics in Amalfi town, and enjoy the Mediterranean breeze through terraced lemon groves.
